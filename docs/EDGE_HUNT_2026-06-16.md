# MARKET RADAR — Edge Hunt, Honest Synthesis (2026-06-16)

**Author:** synthesis pass over 7 independent hunt agents, with the load-bearing numbers
**re-verified from scratch** against `data/market_radar.db`.
**Methodology floor:** `scripts/edge_screen_v2.py` doctrine — dedup to one bet per
`(ticker, scored-day)`; DAY-DEMEAN (subtract that day's mean across all clean deduped bets =
strip market beta); net of realistic round-trip cost (`market_radar.backtest.replay._round_trip_cost_frac`,
20 bps liquid → 400 bps sub-$1); clean rule `COALESCE(data_corrupt,0)=0 AND price_at_flag>=1`,
plus `price<=2000`, `|ret5d|<=100`. Report **median + 10%-trimmed mean + drop-top-N**, not the mean.

## TL;DR — the honest bottom line

**There is no "real" edge in this data.** Nothing cleared the bar of *positive net-of-cost AND
persists across an early/late split AND not driven by a handful of outliers* on a sample large
enough to trust. The data is one ~5-week regime (19 distinct 5d-resolved scored-days, dominated by
a 5,925-bet backfill on 2026-05-12) — structurally too thin and too single-regime to *prove* most
hypotheses either way.

The single most promising idea — **large insider open-market purchases (Form-4 code P)** — is
**fragile, and weaker than the most bullish hunt agent claimed.** When I re-ran it with strict
de-duplication (one bet per ticker-day, no sticky window flag), it (a) loses its statistical
significance (permutation p ≈ 0.075, not the p ≈ 0.003 reported), and (b) is carried by ~3-5 winners
(drop top-3 → +0.12%, drop top-5 → negative). It is worth instrumenting forward; it is NOT worth
sized capital today.

## Why two agents disagreed on the insider edge — and who was right

Agent #2 reported insider P-buys at **n=46, mean +3.24%, median +1.88%, permutation p=0.0029**, and
called it the most promising thing in the dataset. Agent #6, after *de-stickying* (one bet per
purchase event rather than a 30-day window flag that re-counts one Form-4 on up to 7 scored-days),
got a much weaker result that died on a drop-top-3 test.

**I reproduced both and Agent #6 is correct.** The 30-day-window flag is pseudo-replication: it
counts a single insider filing as multiple "bets," which shrinks the permutation null's SD and
manufactures significance. My strict one-bet-per-(ticker,scored-day) re-run:

| insider cut (strict dedup) | n | days | tickers | mean | median | trim10 | drop-3 | drop-5 | win% | early/late mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P (acquired, open-market) | 32 | 13 | 27 | **+1.68%** | **+1.59%** | +1.31% | **+0.12%** | **−0.43%** | 68.8% | +1.16 / +2.54 |
| P + role_score≥2 (senior) | 24 | 13 | 20 | +0.45% | +1.19% | −0.09% | −1.36% | −2.03% | 58.3% | −0.19 / +1.22 |
| P + notional≥$100k | 14 | 10 | 12 | +3.26% | +2.63% | +2.81% | **−0.12%** | −1.53% | 78.6% | +2.02 / +4.91 |

Permutation (within-day random draws, 20k iters, **pre-cost** demeaned, strict dedup):
**observed +2.71% vs null mean −0.00% sd 1.89, p(≥obs) = 0.075.** Not significant.

Price-tier (net-of-cost, strict): the entire signal lives in the >=$50 bucket (n=6, +6.5% mean) and
$10-50 (n=10, +1.45%); sub-$10 is flat/negative. That is the *opposite* of a cost artifact (good),
but n=6 in the load-bearing bucket is not a finding.

**Honest read:** the cruder Lakonishok-Lee "insiders buy = good" tilt is *present at the median* and
*directionally robust*, but it is **outlier-carried and not statistically distinguishable from the
day's universe** once you stop double-counting. The literature's refinements (opportunistic > routine,
clusters > singles, senior > junior — Cohen-Malloy-Pomorski) **did not replicate** here (senior cut
is *worse*; clusters were negative; there's effectively no 10b5-1 control group). So we cannot even
claim the mechanism, only the crude tilt.

## 1. Ranked edge candidates (verified)

Ranked by realistic promise. **None is "real."**

### #1 — Large insider open-market purchases (Form-4 code P) — **FRAGILE**
- **Verified:** strict dedup n=32, 13 days, 27 tickers, median **+1.59%** net 5d, win 68.8%,
  early +1.16% / late +2.54% (both positive). **But** drop-3 → +0.12%, drop-5 → negative;
  permutation **p=0.075** (NS); the $100k sub-slice (n=14) also dies on drop-3 (−0.12%).
- **Why only fragile:** 13 days, one up-tape regime (≤ project's 15-day floor); outlier-carried;
  mechanism refinements failed to replicate; 5d return is an optimistic upper bound (no intraday path).
- **Retail-defensible?** Yes in principle — large-cap optionable names, public free signal (Form-4),
  not crowded the way 13F-copying is. This is the one to instrument forward.

### #2 — 8-K "avoid-long" gate (bearish item codes) — **FRAGILE (as a filter, not a trade)**
- **Verified by agent #5** on the only sub-corpus with parseable item codes (3,030 rows, 20 publish-days):
  bearish-item 8-Ks (delisting 3.01, restatement 4.02, dilution 3.02, impairment 2.06) underperform
  non-bearish in *both* OOS halves; delisting long net-alpha median −5.05%, robust mean −4.64%.
- **The tradeable SHORT fails:** delisting short net-alpha median +0.53% — break-even *before* borrow
  fees, with −72%/+64% squeeze tails on exactly these hard-to-borrow names.
- **Caveats:** capped at one 4-week regime because **94% of historical 8-K bodies are NULL**; per-day
  sign test only 12/20 (barely above coin-flip); effect lives in sub-$5 microcaps. **Use as an
  exclusion filter** ("don't go long into a delisting/restatement/dilution 8-K"), not a short.
- **Real bug found:** `edge_screen_v2.py` and `analyze_8k_items.py` day-demean by `scored_at`
  (a backfill batch date), collapsing ~462 real event-days into ~19. For event-anchored signals
  (8-K, 13D) the demean axis must be `published_at` (== `price_at_flag_ts`). Corrected reference:
  `scripts/edge_8k_drift_honest.py`.

### #3 — Cross-sectional `model_p_5d` decile spread — **FRAGILE → ARTIFACT**
- **Verified by agent #6:** top-decile-minus-bottom +1.03%/day mean **but median only +0.03%**, and
  OOS early −0.02 / late +1.97 — regime-loaded onto the June sub-window. Beta cancels by construction,
  but the spread is a few-name, late-window artifact, not a stable factor.

### #4 — Price-action breakout / abnormal-volume (donchian/ORB/vol-spike) — **ARTIFACT**
- **Verified by agent #4:** r5 net +0.77% to +1.1%/trade, median agrees, win 54-57% — *looks* real,
  but exists on only 11 days and the **entire edge is one 5-day window (June 1-5)**, bracketed by
  negative days on both sides. OOS early-half negative for every breakout bucket. Statistically
  indistinguishable from one good week.

### #5 — Activist 13D initiation drift (Brav-Jiang) — **ARTIFACT**
- **Verified (re-ran with event-day demean on `published_at`):** 13D initiations on liquid (≥$10)
  names, demeaned against the 13D cohort: **1d mean −1.13% / median −0.72% / win 38%;
  5d −2.93%; 20d −3.55%.** The predicted multi-week drift is *absent/negative*. The "1d pop"
  agent #3 salvaged (~+0.9% median vs the broad 13x pool) does **not** survive a clean
  same-cohort event-day demean. n=287 initiations but all from one 2024-H2 backfill, zero live confirmation.

### #6 — PEAD / earnings-surprise drift — **ARTIFACT (5d) / UNTESTABLE_YET (20d)**
- **Verified by agents #1 & #6:** `earnings_beat` px≥5 5d mean +0.92% is a textbook trap — median
  −0.09%, trim10 ≈ 0%, win 49%, top-5 names are +57/+50/+44/+44/+39%; drop-10 → negative; OOS
  *flips sign* (early +2.75% → late −0.52%). The canonical PEAD horizon (20-60 days) has 20d outcomes
  on only 4 days → **the test that would matter is unrunnable.** `eps_surprise_pct` is denominator
  junk (−6947% to +34929%); standardized SUE isn't computable from this schema.

### #7 — Options as leverage — **ARTIFACT (no viable expression today)**
- **Verified by agent #7** on a *live* ABBV chain: a 220/230 bull-call debit spread costs $4.04 mid;
  round-trip crossing the spread burns **21% of the debit**. The measured ~0.5% underlying "edge" moves
  the spread ~+8% of debit. Net ≈ **−13%/trade.** Options convert a near-zero edge into a reliably
  negative one. The repo's coded 10% option RT assumption is ~2× too optimistic vs the real 21%.

### Untestable (substrate gaps — not artifacts, just no data)
- **Short-squeeze (H5):** `short_interest` is effectively one snapshot, `short_pct_float` NULL; `fails_to_deliver` is stale 2020 data.
- **Under-covered-microcap drift (H4):** *wrong sign* here — cheap catalyst-chasers (px<$5) reliably
  underperform their own day (−3 to −4% net), monotone and outlier-robust. Un-shortable for retail
  → it's an **exclusion filter**, not a trade.

## 2. System design — forward validation that plugs into the clean ingestion

The whole problem is sample. The system must turn each future day into a *true* out-of-sample day
and judge on the honest statistics, not the mean.

1. **`edge_ledger` table** (new): on every scored-day, snapshot each candidate cohort's bets keyed by
   `(candidate, ticker, scored_day, event_day, price_at_flag, score_id)`. Candidates v1:
   `insider_P_acquired`, `insider_P_notional_ge_100k`, `bearish_8k_avoid_long`. Append-only; this *is*
   the forward OOS record.
2. **Demean-axis fix:** for event-anchored signals (8-K, 13D, Form-4), demean on `published_at`
   (== `price_at_flag_ts`), not `scored_at`. Carry a `cost_bucket` column so the early/late split can't
   be gamed by the 2026-05-12 microcap-backfill cost-composition confound that fooled the naive split.
3. **Strict dedup is mandatory** — one bet per `(ticker, scored-day)`, no sticky window flags. (The
   sticky flag is exactly what manufactured the false p=0.003.)
4. **Promotion gate (`fragile → real`):** require, on **≥15 NEW distinct live days** excluding the
   05-12 backfill **and** spanning ≥2 market weeks/regimes: net-of-cost **median > 0** AND **trim10 > 0**
   AND **drop-top-5 still > 0** AND **positive in both early and late halves** AND **permutation p < 0.05
   on the strictly-deduped sample**. Anything that needs the mean but not the median stays fragile.
5. **Defined-risk sizing (only after promotion):** per-name risk ≤ 0.5% of equity; hard stop at
   −1.5×ATR; **stock only**, ≥$10 price (the tier where the insider tilt isn't a cost artifact); max
   5 concurrent positions; daily loss limit. No options until a directional edge clears the option
   bid/ask (it does not today).
6. **Substrate to unblock the untestable:** ingest a short-interest *time series* with
   `short_pct_float`; refresh FTDs; backfill 8-K bodies (94% NULL) so the item-conditioned sign test
   gets a multi-regime sample.

## 3. Options verdict

**Freeze the options bot.** Not a liquidity problem (the surviving edge families are largely large-cap
optionable) — it's that **no proven directional edge is large enough to clear the option bid/ask
(~21% RT on a real ABBV vertical).** Selling premium is worse: uncapped fat-tail loss on the exact
catalyst names this system flags (earnings/M&A/FDA gap risk) wipes a small account on one event.
Options are reconsiderable **only if** the insider or upgrade cohort clears the promotion gate on
$50+ names across a second regime — then the *narrowest* 30-45 DTE ATM debit spread, mid-or-better
limit fills, single mega-cap underlyings. Not before.

## 4. Income path to £150/day — the real math

£150/day is a **capital milestone, not a return on £5k.** Even the *unproven* best-case ~+0.5%/trade
trimmed stock edge implies you need roughly **£75k-£150k of working capital** (or a funded prop seat)
to target £150/day, and that assumes the edge survives forward — which on this data it has **not** been
shown to. On £5k the same edge is ~£9/day, and that's before the honest haircut that the edge is
fragile/outlier-carried. **Do not size capital against any number in this report.** The path is:
(1) prove the insider tilt forward to the promotion gate on paper; (2) only then pilot tiny *stock*;
(3) the capital, not cleverness, is what gets you to £150/day — or a prop allocation that someone else
funds against a *proven* track record.

## 5. The single most promising thing to instrument next

**Stand up the `edge_ledger` forward paper-track for large insider open-market buys (Form-4 code P,
acquired, ≥$10 price), logging each fresh purchase's scored-day, event-day-demeaned net-of-cost 5d AND
20d return, with strict one-bet-per-(ticker,day) dedup.** It is the only candidate that is
theory-backed, retail-defensible, positive at the median, and not a cost or beta artifact — it just
needs ≥15 *new* OOS days in a non-trending regime and a drop-top-5 that stays positive before it can be
promoted from *fragile* to *real*. Everything else is an artifact or untestable until the substrate
gaps (short-interest time series, 8-K bodies, 20d earnings outcomes) are filled.
